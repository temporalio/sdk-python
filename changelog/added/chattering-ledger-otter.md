Add the experimental Streams API. Client and Activity code create a Stream, append Records to it, read it and close it. A client takes its Stream Stores in `stream_config=StreamConfig(stores=[...])`, such as `RedisStreamStore` of the `temporalio-streams-redis` package, or `temporalio.testing.MemoryStreamStore` in tests.
Add `temporalio.contrib.streams.current_producer` and `as_producer`. An Activity can store its Producer in its heartbeat details and pass it to the appends of its retry. The retry's Records then continue the first attempt's epoch, so a reader does not take them as a replacement of the first attempt's Records.
A Workflow outbound interceptor sees a Stream operation only in its operation-specific method, such as `start_append_records`, and not in `start_system_nexus_operation`. An unknown Stream operation raises `ValueError`.
