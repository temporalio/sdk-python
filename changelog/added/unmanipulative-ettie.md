Core: Experimental session workers can poll workflow-affine activity queues; individual activities opt into their workflow's session, while normal activity routing remains the default. This increment supports fixed-size activity slots with capacity of at least two; resource-based and custom activity suppliers are not supported yet. Activity session options must select workflow-session routing; empty options or an explicit false selection are rejected.
Core: Experimental worker session options support a single-session policy with a stable destination, dedicating that destination to the first matched workflow for its lifetime with a supporting server. Sticky-only mode may use one fixed activity slot.
